TORN ARCADE
A phone full of games for Torn. Completely free.
Version 2.577 gamesThe Legends folderNo API key needed

Torn Arcade adds a Mobile Phone link to Torn's sidebar. Click it and a phone opens over the page, full of games: a creature-collecting RPG, a slingshot game, tower defence, a rooftop runner, a Catan-style board game, chess, Flappy Torn and, in the Legends folder, a full role-playing game. Everything plays on your own device. No game needs an API key, an account or a server.

New in 2.57: Legend of Aethra

A 16-bit style role-playing game inspired by the classic Final Fantasy games, in the Legends folder on the home screen. A newly sworn Warden takes his first contract, to get a band of rebels into a reactor and out again, and falls into a story about stolen memories, four Crystals that hold up the sky, and the woman who wants all four.

  • Five acts, about 40 hours, with eight playable characters and an ending that remembers who you brought along.
  • Active-time battles: 154 monsters and 52 bosses, Prisms to slot spells and skills, Surge Arts and Echo summons.
  • An open 3D world map you cross on foot, by talonbird, skimmer, boat, submersible and airship, with hidden places, a ghost ship and secrets to find.
  • Side content: the Arcana card game, talonbird races, an arena, side quests, super-bosses and New Game+.
  • An original soundtrack: 45 tracks, with a theme for every town. It plays inside the phone with a keyboard or the on-screen touch pad.
Before you install Legend of Aethra: large scripts.
The game is about 2.2 MB and its optional Soundtrack about 16 MB, much bigger than a usual userscript. They can take a moment to install and to load, and some mobile browsers and userscript managers may not be able to install or run scripts this size. If the Soundtrack won't install or slows your phone down, leave it out: the game still plays, with sound effects but no music. A PC or laptop is the most reliable way to play.

Install it from inside the Legends folder (the game and its Soundtrack sit side by side), or here: Legend of Aethra and its Soundtrack. Legend of Varrowmere is taking a short break from the Legends folder.

The games
Tornies
A creature-collecting RPG set in Torn City. Catch and train 178 Tornies, win 8 Arena medals, beat the Torn Council and the Champion. Day and night, weather, fishing, eggs, golden Tornies, a Battle Tower, puzzle Arenas and safe online trades.
Angry Bogies
Bogie versus the Racket. Fling Bogies from the slingshot into the gang's hideouts and knock out every thug. Fifteen levels, three stars each.
Faction Defence
Tower defence. Build and upgrade five kinds of defender to hold your Faction HQ against 30 waves of Racket raiders, on 6 maps.
Anton's Egg Dash
An endless rooftop runner. Anton, in his pink bunny onesie, runs from the police with a sack of Easter eggs, and it keeps getting faster.
Torn Territories
A district-building board game by the rules of Catan, with the Flight Paths expansion (Seafarers rules). Play the computer, pass-and-play, or online against other players.
Flappy Torn
Tap to fly through the pipes over the Torn City skyline. One difficulty for everyone, so every score counts on the same leaderboard.
Chess
Play the computer on three levels, or play online with a 45 second move clock, public games and Quick match. Full rules, and an online Chess rating.
Leaderboards and Settings
Your best scores on this device, and optional global leaderboards. Phone themes, wallpapers, volume and timed reminders.
How to install
  1. Install the Tampermonkey browser extension.
  2. Open the Torn Arcade install link and click Install.
  3. Open Torn and click Mobile Phone under Calendar in the sidebar. On pages without the sidebar, a small floating Phone button appears instead.

Your progress is saved in Tampermonkey's storage on your device, and updates install themselves. On a keyboard, Esc is the back button.

Torn's scripting rules
  • Never touches Torn's page. Apart from the Mobile Phone link, it never reads, clicks or changes anything in Torn. (Optional: Torn chat stays usable over the phone, a display change only.)
  • No non-API requests to Torn, it never loads Torn pages, and it uses no websockets.
  • The only websites it talks to are the official Torn API (only if you add a key) and, only with Online play on, the Torn Arcade server.
  • The Art Packs, Legend of Aethra and its Soundtrack make no requests at all. They talk to the phone through page events only.
  • The full source is on GitHub, with no hidden code.
